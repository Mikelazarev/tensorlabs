Careers and Open Positions

At TensorLabs we are looking for machine learning and deep learning experts who want to work at the intersection of AI and medicine: computer vision for medical images and natural language processing for dialogue with patients.

How we work together:
  • Join the TensorLabs team — developing and deploying applied AI solutions for healthcare
  • Research collaborations and internships on our clinical projects
If there is no matching position, write to us anyway: we are always looking for people who share our goals.

ML/DL Expert in Computer Vision

Where: TensorLabs
Responsibilities:
  • Develop and train computer vision models for medical image analysis: facial photographs, coronary angiography
  • Vessel segmentation and object reconstruction from X-ray projections
  • Medical data preparation and quality control, reproducible preprocessing pipelines
  • Validation and deployment of algorithms in pilot projects with clinics
Requirements:
  • Strong Python and PyTorch
  • Experience in CV tasks: segmentation, classification, regression; vision transformers
  • Understanding of medical data specifics and clinical validation metrics is a plus
  • Portfolio/publications are welcome
Conditions: discussed individually.
    To apply, email neurofacies@gmail.com

ML/DL Expert in NLP

Where: TensorLabs
Responsibilities:
  • Dialogue systems for post-operative follow-up: extracting structured patient answers from speech and text
  • Working with large language models: prompting, fine-tuning, local models, quality and reliability evaluation
  • Speech recognition and synthesis inside the product, multimodal data
  • Participation in clinical pilots and research projects, publications
Requirements:
  • Experience with transformers and LLMs (PyTorch, Hugging Face, etc.)
  • Understanding of language model architectures, information extraction and dialogue evaluation
  • Experience with speech technologies (ASR/TTS) is a plus
  • Portfolio/publications are welcome
Conditions: discussed individually.
